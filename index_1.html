HR & PAYROLL AUTOMATION

Mini HRIS System

A Google Workspace–based HR system that replaces manual attendance sheets, overtime logs, and payroll calculation with one connected workflow — built as a simulation of a real small-company HRIS.

The problem

Attendance, overtime, and payroll were tracked manually and separately, which meant slow processing, inconsistent data between sheets, and payroll calculations prone to human error.

What I built

Google Forms for attendance, overtime requests, and new-employee data feeding into a structured Sheets backend (separated raw vs. processed data). ArrayFormula and VLOOKUP automatically calculate lateness, overtime hours, and full payroll — base salary, allowances, bonuses, overtime pay, minus late-penalty deductions. Autocrat + Google Slides auto-generate printable ID cards and payslips, feeding an HR monitoring dashboard.

Result

Cuts processing time by an estimated 70%, removes manual re-entry between sheets, reduces administrative errors, and scales to a larger headcount without redesigning the system — built modular enough to extend into leave, reimbursement, or performance review tracking later.

  • Google Sheets (Advanced)
  • Google Forms
  • Google Slides
  • Autocrat
  • ArrayFormula / VLOOKUP
  • Dashboard Design

Full documented case study, workflow diagrams & dashboard preview available on request.

SMALL-BUSINESS FINANCE TRACKING

Sistem Keuangan "Teh Prabu"

A daily cash-flow tracking system for a small beverage (es & minuman) business, built so a non-technical operator can log every transaction in seconds while the backend stays structured and analysis-ready.

The problem

Daily income and expenses — split across cash and QRIS payments, plus itemized stock, operational, and savings spending — were being tracked informally, making it hard to see real daily profit or where money was going.

What I built

A Google Form for daily transaction entry (income/expense, cash vs. QRIS split, itemized notes) that logs directly into a structured Google Sheets ledger — separating income, itemized expenses, savings allocation, and operational costs per day.

Result

Live, real transaction data logged daily since July 2026, with a clean structure ready for weekly/monthly summary reporting — the same reconciliation discipline shown in the Mini HRIS System, applied here to a real small business.

  • Google Forms
  • Google Sheets
  • Data Reconciliation
