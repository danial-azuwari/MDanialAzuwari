SCHOOL FINANCE & PAYROLL AUTOMATION

Sistem Keuangan Sekolah

An Apps Script–automated finance system for a school with two units (MA & MTS) — logging every transaction, tracking two separate funding sources, and checking teacher payroll automatically every day.

The Problem

The school runs two units — MA and MTS — each receiving two separate funding sources (Dana BOS and SPP), which had to be recorded, categorized, and reported separately. On top of that, teacher pay is calculated per-hour and split across both units, so getting an accurate daily balance and correct payroll meant a lot of manual cross-checking.

What I Built / My Role

A Google Form for transaction entry — separating income (by fund category: BOS or SPP) from itemized expenses — feeding into a protected Google Sheets backend. Master data sheets (M_Unit, M_Guru) hold the two school units and each teacher's subjects, hourly rate, and hours taught per unit, which the system uses to calculate salary automatically.

How It Works / Workflow

Every form submission runs an Apps Script trigger (onFormSubmitTransaksi) that auto-generates a unique transaction ID (e.g. TRX-20260820-0001) and logs it into the transaction database. The core sheets (DB_Transaksi, DB_Gaji, DB_Dana, M_Kategori, M_Set) are locked with sheet protection so daily data entry can't accidentally break the formulas underneath.

A daily time-driven trigger runs at 01:00 to automatically check teacher payroll (jalankanCekGajiHarian) and refresh the reconciliation report (updateLaporan_), broken down by period, unit, and fund type. A live dashboard sheet (Dash) then shows the current balance and income/expense summary at a glance — the same numbers shown on the card back on the portfolio page.

Tools

  • Google Sheets
  • Google Forms
  • Google Apps Script
  • Time-driven Triggers
  • Sheet Protection / Permissions

Result / Outcome

Live and in active use — transactions have been logging automatically with auto-generated IDs since August 2026, covering both units and both fund sources without manual sorting. Teacher payroll is checked automatically every day instead of calculated by hand at the end of the month.
